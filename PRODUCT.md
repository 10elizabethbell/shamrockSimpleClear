# Product: Shamrock Transportation website (simple / clear)

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one page; the visitor decides to call)

## Users
Snowbirds and their families, roughly 40–70, who fly between New Jersey and
Florida each season and need their car moved. They read on a phone, often from a
link opened inside Facebook. They are deciding whether to trust a stranger with
their car. (Audience from the Muse brief and Ellie, 2026-10-13/14.)

## Purpose
Turn a business card into a page that earns one phone call to Ed. Second job:
win over Ed himself, who has no web presence at all.

## Positioning
A one-man driveaway service. Ed Brannagan drives the customer's own car between
New Jersey and Florida, so it can go packed full of winter things, unlike a
trailer carrier.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact is by phone: 732-300-3835, Ed's cell. Texting is unknown, so call is
  the only action on the page.
- No email, Facebook, reviews, photos, logo file, hours, insurance or years in
  business are known. None appear on the page.
- Price: $1 a mile (Ed, via Ellie, 2026-10-15, recorded on the sibling builds).
  What it includes is unknown.

## Brand commitments
- Name: Shamrock Transportation. Owner/driver: Edward (Ed) Brannagan.
- Card services, verbatim: "Local & Long Distance", "Airport Services Anywhere In
  Florida", "Very Reasonable Rates".
- Card tagline, verbatim: "Life is a Highway... Drive it All Night Long".
- Green shamrock emblem on the card (not cleared for public use; a drawn
  shamrock stands in). Simple readable green on white.

## Evidence on hand
Business card facts only (via Ellie's photos, 2026-10-13). No photos, reviews or
listings exist publicly.

## Principles
1. Respect the reader's attention: one idea per screen, nothing to figure out.
2. Nothing moves, nothing hidden, no popups, no sticky widgets.
3. Large type (18px+ body), high contrast, plain short sentences.
4. The phone number is big and tappable wherever a decision happens.
5. Never invent a fact; unknowns stay off the page and go in HANDOFF.md.
